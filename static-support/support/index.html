Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a memorial?

After signing up, tap "Create Memorial" and follow the guided steps to add photos, stories, and details about your loved one. You can save your progress and return anytime.

Can I share a memorial with family and friends?

Yes! Each memorial has sharing options that let you invite others via email, text, or social media. You can control privacy settings for each memorial.

How do QR codes work?

QR codes can be placed on headstones, memorial plaques, or keepsakes. When scanned, they instantly open the digital memorial, allowing visitors to view photos, stories, and leave condolences.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your memories. Your data is backed up securely and will be preserved for future generations.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can manage your subscription through your device's app store settings, or contact our support team and we'll assist you.
